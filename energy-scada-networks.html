SCADA Networks

Real-time supervisory control and data acquisition infrastructure for mission-critical power operations

Enterprise SCADA Architecture for Power Infrastructure

Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition (SCADA) systems form the nervous system of modern power utilities. These distributed control networks enable operators to monitor and control thousands of geographically dispersed assets — from generating units and circuit breakers to transformers and capacitor banks — in real-time from centralized or regional control centers.

Infrasys specializes in designing resilient, scalable, and secure SCADA networks that meet the demanding requirements of power generation, transmission, and distribution operations. Our solutions integrate Human-Machine Interfaces (HMI), Remote Terminal Units (RTUs), Programmable Logic Controllers (PLCs), and Intelligent Electronic Devices (IEDs) into cohesive monitoring and control platforms.

We understand that SCADA networks must balance the conflicting demands of operational availability (99.99%+ uptime), cyber-physical security, protocol interoperability, and low-latency control loops. Our implementations are grounded in electrical substation engineering principles, industrial networking standards, and operational technology best practices developed through real-world deployments.

Communication Infrastructure

Modern SCADA systems rely on diverse communication media to maintain connectivity across vast geographical areas. Our designs incorporate redundant communication paths to ensure continuous data flow even when primary links fail:

  • Fiber Optic Networks: Self-healing ring topology using OPGW (Optical Ground Wire) on transmission towers or dedicated dark fiber for low-latency, high-bandwidth SCADA traffic
  • Microwave Radio Links: Point-to-point and point-to-multipoint licensed microwave systems for remote substations where fiber deployment is cost-prohibitive
  • Cellular Networks (4G/LTE): Backup communication paths using private APN or MPLS-based cellular connectivity with managed security policies
  • Satellite Communication: VSAT terminals for remote generation sites, ensuring connectivity in locations beyond terrestrial network reach
  • Serial Communication: RS-232, RS-485, and Current Loop interfaces for legacy RTU connectivity and brownfield modernization

Protocol Stack & Standards

We design SCADA systems that speak the language of your existing and future infrastructure. Our protocol engineering capabilities span the full spectrum of industrial control system standards:

DNP3 (Distributed Network Protocol 3) IEC 61850 (Substation Automation) Modbus TCP/RTU IEC 60870-5-104 OPC-UA (Unified Architecture) ICCP/TASE.2 (Inter-Control Center)

Each protocol implementation is rigorously tested using protocol analyzers and simulation environments before field deployment. We handle conformance testing, interoperability validation, and performance optimization to ensure your SCADA network operates reliably under normal and stressed conditions.

SCADA Network Security Architecture

The convergence of SCADA systems with corporate IT networks introduces significant cybersecurity challenges. We implement defense-in-depth strategies aligned with NERC CIP (Critical Infrastructure Protection) and IEC 62443 standards:

  • Network Segmentation: Physical and logical separation of control networks using industrial firewalls, unidirectional gateways, and VLANs
  • Access Control: Role-based access control (RBAC), multi-factor authentication, and privileged access management for SCADA operators and engineers
  • Intrusion Detection: OT-aware intrusion detection systems (IDS) with protocol-specific anomaly detection for DNP3, Modbus, and IEC 61850 traffic
  • Security Monitoring: 24x7 security operations center (SOC) integration with SIEM correlation rules for SCADA-specific threat patterns
  • Patch Management: Structured vulnerability management and patching processes that minimize operational disruption while maintaining security posture

Historian & Data Analytics Integration

Beyond real-time control, SCADA systems generate vast amounts of operational data that drives predictive maintenance, regulatory reporting, and business intelligence. We integrate SCADA platforms with industrial historians and analytics tools:

Time-series databases (OSIsoft PI, GE Proficy, Wonderware) capture high-frequency measurements from thousands of data points. This historical data feeds asset performance management systems, enabling utilities to transition from reactive to predictive maintenance strategies.

Real-time analytics engines detect operational anomalies — voltage excursions, harmonic distortions, abnormal load patterns — and trigger automated corrective actions or operator alerts. Machine learning models trained on historical SCADA data predict equipment failures days or weeks in advance, optimizing maintenance schedules and minimizing forced outages.

SCADA Modernization & Migration

Many power utilities operate aging SCADA systems with end-of-life hardware, obsolete operating systems, and limited vendor support. We specialize in phased SCADA modernization that maintains operational continuity:

  • Assessment & Roadmap: Comprehensive audit of existing SCADA infrastructure, identifying risks, constraints, and modernization priorities
  • Parallel Operations: New SCADA platform deployed alongside legacy system, with gradual migration of substations to minimize operational risk
  • Protocol Translation: Gateway devices and protocol converters enable coexistence of legacy and modern devices during transition periods
  • Operator Training: Hands-on training programs ensure control room staff are proficient with new HMI interfaces and alarm management workflows
  • Cutover Planning: Meticulously planned cutover sequences with rollback procedures, minimizing impact on power system operations
