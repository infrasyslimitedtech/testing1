Real-Time Asset Tracking

IoT-enabled monitoring for containers, pallets, and high-value shipments across the supply chain

IoT-Powered Supply Chain Visibility

In global supply chains spanning ocean freight, rail transport, trucking, and air cargo, maintaining visibility of assets in transit has traditionally been a black box. Shipments disappear into containers, trailers, and warehouses with limited insight into location, condition, or handling until they emerge at the next checkpoint. This lack of visibility creates uncertainty, increases inventory buffers, delays exception detection, and leaves high-value or sensitive shipments vulnerable to theft, damage, or mishandling.

Real-time asset tracking using IoT sensors and wireless connectivity transforms this opacity into continuous visibility. Smart tracking devices attached to containers, pallets, parcels, and individual products transmit location coordinates, environmental conditions, and security events throughout the journey—from factory gate to destination warehouse—enabling proactive exception management and end-to-end supply chain transparency.

Infrasys designs and implements enterprise asset tracking solutions combining GPS/GNSS positioning, cellular and satellite connectivity, low-power wide-area network (LPWAN) technologies, and cloud analytics platforms. Our implementations serve freight forwarders, third-party logistics providers, manufacturers with high-value products, cold chain operators, and companies shipping regulated or hazardous materials requiring continuous compliance monitoring.

Core Asset Tracking Capabilities

Multi-Network Connectivity

GPS tracking with cellular (4G/LTE/NB-IoT), satellite (Iridium, Globalstar), and LPWAN (LoRaWAN, Sigfox) ensuring global coverage across ocean, remote, and urban environments.

Environmental Monitoring

Temperature, humidity, shock, tilt, light exposure, and vibration sensors protecting pharmaceuticals, fresh produce, electronics, and other sensitive cargo from environmental damage.

Security & Tamper Detection

Door sensors, tamper alerts, unauthorized movement detection, and geofence breach notifications protecting high-value shipments from theft and diversion.

Long-Life Battery Design

Optimized power management enabling multi-year battery life for reusable trackers on returnable assets like containers, pallets, and shipping cages.

Our asset tracking platforms process millions of location updates, sensor readings, and alert events daily, correlating device telemetry with shipment data, route plans, and exception thresholds. Real-time dashboards provide logistics managers with visual maps showing asset locations, predicted ETAs, exception alerts, and historical movement trails. API integrations feed tracking data into transportation management systems (TMS), enterprise resource planning (ERP) platforms, and customer portals providing end-to-end supply chain visibility.

GPS & Positioning Technologies

Accurate location determination is the foundation of asset tracking. Our tracking devices utilize multi-constellation global navigation satellite systems (GNSS) including GPS, GLONASS, Galileo, and BeiDou for enhanced accuracy and reliability especially in challenging environments like urban canyons or under building overhangs.

For indoor environments where satellite signals are unavailable—inside warehouses, distribution centers, or intermodal facilities—we deploy complementary positioning technologies. Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) beacons enable room-level indoor positioning. Wi-Fi fingerprinting uses signal strength patterns from access points for location estimation. Ultra-wideband (UWB) technology provides centimeter-level positioning accuracy for high-precision applications.

Positioning frequency is configurable based on application requirements and power constraints. High-value shipments may report location every 10-15 minutes during transit. Long-haul ocean containers might report twice daily via satellite to preserve battery. Event-triggered positioning activates upon geofence entry/exit, shock detection, or temperature excursion, ensuring critical events generate immediate location data.

Cold Chain & Temperature Monitoring

Temperature-sensitive products—pharmaceuticals, biologics, vaccines, fresh produce, frozen foods, chemicals—require continuous temperature monitoring throughout the cold chain. Brief excursions above acceptable ranges can render products unsaleable or unsafe, resulting in costly rejections and regulatory violations.

Our cold chain tracking solutions combine calibrated temperature sensors, real-time alerts, and compliance reporting. Configurable temperature thresholds trigger alerts when readings drift outside acceptable ranges. Alert escalation workflows notify logistics coordinators, warehouse managers, and quality assurance teams enabling rapid intervention—adjusting refrigeration equipment, rerouting shipments, or expediting deliveries before product integrity is compromised.

Multi-point temperature sensing distinguishes between ambient conditions and product temperature, critical for refrigerated containers where air temperature may differ from product core temperature. Data logger functionality maintains complete temperature history even when connectivity is unavailable, downloadable upon shipment completion for regulatory compliance documentation and insurance claims.

Integration with blockchain platforms provides immutable cold chain records, demonstrating unbroken chain of custody and continuous temperature compliance for pharmaceutical supply chains subject to GDP (Good Distribution Practice), FDA 21 CFR Part 11, and other regulatory standards.

Shock, Tilt, & Handling Monitoring

Fragile, high-value, or precision equipment requires careful handling throughout the supply chain. Multi-axis accelerometers detect shock events (sudden impacts), excessive vibration, and improper orientation (tilting packages marked "This Side Up"). Event-based alerts notify logistics managers when rough handling occurs, enabling investigation and carrier accountability.

Historical shock and vibration data supports damage claims, providing objective evidence of mishandling events correlated with timestamps and locations. This data strengthens insurance claims and helps identify problematic transfer points, carriers, or handling facilities where training interventions or process improvements are needed.

For shipments containing lithium batteries, dangerous goods, or other hazardous materials, shock monitoring supports regulatory compliance demonstrating shipments were handled according to transportation regulations (IATA, DOT, ADR) throughout the journey.

Security & Theft Prevention

High-value shipments—electronics, pharmaceuticals, luxury goods, precious metals—are vulnerable to cargo theft, container diversion, and unauthorized access. Security-focused tracking devices include tamper detection, door sensors, and light sensors detecting unauthorized container opening.

Geofence management defines virtual boundaries around authorized routes and facilities. Alerts trigger when shipments deviate from expected paths, remain stationary at unauthorized locations, or move during scheduled rest periods. Real-time alerting enables rapid response—contacting carriers, notifying law enforcement, or remotely immobilizing vehicles through integration with fleet management systems.

For returnable assets like shipping containers, pallets, IBCs (intermediate bulk containers), and reusable packaging, tracking prevents "shrinkage"—assets disappearing into customer facilities or diversion to grey markets. Automated asset recovery workflows trigger when containers exceed expected dwell times at customer sites or fail to return to designated depots.

Satellite Tracking for Ocean & Remote Assets

Cellular connectivity supports most asset tracking applications, but two scenarios require satellite communication—ocean freight crossing international waters beyond cellular coverage, and shipments through remote regions lacking terrestrial infrastructure.

Our satellite tracking solutions utilize Iridium, Globalstar, and Inmarsat satellite networks providing global coverage including polar regions, oceans, deserts, and mountainous terrain. Satellite transmission costs are higher than cellular, so our devices intelligently optimize reporting frequency and data payload size preserving battery life while maintaining necessary visibility.

Hybrid devices combine cellular and satellite connectivity, preferring lower-cost cellular when available and automatically switching to satellite when cellular signals are unavailable. This provides seamless global coverage while minimizing satellite air-time costs.

Low-Power Wide-Area Networks (LPWAN)

For applications requiring years of battery life tracking assets within metropolitan or regional areas—rental equipment, construction tools, retail display fixtures, medical equipment—LPWAN technologies provide optimal power efficiency.

LoRaWAN networks operated by telecommunications providers or private networks deployed on warehouses and distribution centers enable battery-powered tracking devices operating 5-10 years on a single battery. NB-IoT (Narrowband IoT) provides similar battery life using licensed cellular spectrum with better penetration into buildings.

Trade-offs exist—LPWAN networks transmit less data less frequently than cellular connections. For applications where hourly location updates suffice and detailed sensor telemetry is unnecessary, LPWAN provides compelling total cost of ownership advantages through extended battery life and lower connectivity costs.

Integration & API Ecosystem

Asset tracking devices generate value when their data flows into operational systems guiding decisions and actions. Our tracking platforms expose RESTful APIs enabling integration with diverse enterprise systems:

  • Transportation Management Systems (TMS): Real-time shipment location data updates estimated arrival times, automatically advancing shipment milestones, and triggering customer notifications
  • Warehouse Management Systems (WMS): Inbound shipment visibility enables dock scheduling optimization, labor planning, and automated receiving workflows
  • Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP): In-transit inventory visibility improves order promising accuracy and enables dynamic inventory allocation decisions
  • Customer Portals: White-label tracking pages and notification APIs provide customers with real-time shipment visibility, predictive ETAs, and exception alerts
  • Business Intelligence (BI) Tools: Historical tracking data feeds dashboards and reports analyzing transit time performance, carrier service quality, and supply chain network optimization
