Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often from new clients.

How quickly can Infrasys start on my project?

For most engagements, we can mobilise a discovery team within 5–10 business days of contract signing. Complex programmes may require 2–3 weeks for full team assembly.

Do you work with small and mid-sized businesses, or only enterprise?

We work across the spectrum — from SMEs building their first proper data centre strategy to global enterprises managing multi-country infrastructure programmes.

Is the initial consultation free?

Yes. We offer a complimentary 60-minute discovery call where we listen to your challenges and provide an initial perspective on the best approach — with no obligation.

Which geographies does Infrasys operate in?

We are currently active in India, UAE, UK, Singapore, Kenya, and Australia. We can support remote engagements globally and deploy on-site teams in most markets with sufficient notice.

How is pricing structured?

We offer fixed-fee project engagements, time-and-materials retainers, and outcome-based contracts depending on the scope. We'll discuss the best model for your project during the initial consultation.

Prefer to talk directly?

Call our team on +91 124 495 9639 or email info@infrasys.tech — we're here Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM IST.
