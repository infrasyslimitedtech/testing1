  • JavaScript / React for frontend UI development
  • Experience with bench, Linux server management
  • Understanding of accounting, inventory, and HR business processes
  • Git version control and collaborative development

Experience & Compensation

  • Preferred: Less than 3 years of experience
  • Salary based on quality of experience and current / last drawn salary
  • Competitive benefits package as per company policy

Salesforce Developer
1 Opening  ·  Gurugram, Haryana (On-site)
Full-Time Gurugram, NCR Actively Hiring Based on Experience

About the Role

We are seeking an experienced Salesforce Developer to join our growing team at Infrasys. You will be responsible for designing, developing, and implementing customized Salesforce solutions for enterprise clients across various industries. This role requires strong technical expertise in Salesforce development and a passion for delivering high-quality CRM solutions.

Key Responsibilities

  • Design and develop custom Salesforce applications using Apex, Visualforce, and Lightning Web Components (LWC)
  • Configure and customize Salesforce objects, fields, workflows, process builder, and validation rules
  • Integrate Salesforce with third-party applications using REST/SOAP APIs
  • Develop and maintain triggers, batch classes, and scheduled jobs in Apex
  • Implement and customize Salesforce features including Sales Cloud, Service Cloud, and Experience Cloud
  • Perform data migration, data loading, and data quality management using Data Loader and other ETL tools
  • Create comprehensive technical documentation and design specifications
  • Collaborate with business analysts and stakeholders to gather requirements and deliver solutions
  • Provide technical support and troubleshooting for Salesforce applications
  • Follow Salesforce best practices and maintain code quality standards

Required Skills & Experience

  • 3-4 years of hands-on Salesforce development experience
  • Strong proficiency in Apex, Visualforce, Lightning Web Components (LWC), and Aura Components
  • Experience with Salesforce declarative tools (Flows, Process Builder, Workflow Rules)
  • Knowledge of Salesforce APIs (REST, SOAP, Bulk API) and integration patterns
  • Understanding of Salesforce security model, sharing rules, and profiles/permissions
  • Experience with version control systems (Git) and deployment tools (Change Sets, ANT, SFDX)
  • Salesforce Platform Developer I certification (mandatory)
  • Salesforce Administrator or Platform Developer II certification (preferred)
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
  • Excellent communication and client-facing skills

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience with Salesforce CPQ, Marketing Cloud, or other Salesforce products
  • Knowledge of JavaScript frameworks (React, Angular) and modern web development
  • Experience working in Agile/Scrum development environment
  • Understanding of DevOps practices for Salesforce

Location & Compensation

  • Location: Gurugram, Haryana (On-site)
  • Experience: 3-4 years required
  • Compensation: Based on experience and current salary
  • Benefits: Competitive package as per company policy
